# Design

Operate surface for a basketball coach reviewing synchronized spatial decisions. Preserve the established gold route logo, dark identity, blue offense and red defense.

The court is the dominant artifact: graphite NBA-proportioned playing surface by default, optional maple, precise neutral markings, warm hoop, distinct jerseys and a low-contrast arena surround. Color is functional: blue movement, gold possession/pass/screen, red defensive rotation, translucent blue created space.

Use one charcoal/steel panel system, 5–10px radii, warm gold active controls, readable analysis type, tabular timecode and restrained borders. Prefer open analysis rows over nested cards. Compact header, left library, central board plus timeline, right coverage and coaching notes; collapsible panels and stacked mobile layout.

Motion comes from the tactical scene: arc-length curves, acceleration/deceleration, contact pauses, rotating bodies, pass/catch windows, shot arc and camera focus. Everything derives from absolute playback time. Reduced-motion disables decorative entrance/transition motion; tactical playback remains explicitly user controlled.

Foundation tactics are labeled separately from the authored advanced PnR. No fabricated metrics, real-game reconstruction claims or player likenesses.
